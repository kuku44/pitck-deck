Slide 2: The Problem
Headline: Mobility in Kakuma is Broken.
Unsafe: No trip tracking or driver vetting, leaving vulnerable populations (refugees, aid workers, families) at high risk.
Unpredictable: Informal fares fluctuate wildly; price gouging is common and unregulated.
Exclusive: Standard rideshare apps fail here due to low smartphone ownership, expensive data, and intermittent connectivity.
Slide 3: The Solution & Product
Headline: Built for the Realities of Turkana.
Dual Access: Lightweight app + USSD (*123#) for basic phones. Zero internet required to book.
Verified Drivers: Strict vetting and safety training in partnership with local transport unions.
Cashless & Transparent: Seamless M-Pesa integration eliminates cash disputes and ensures predictable pricing.
Built-in Safety: 1-tap booking for riders, low-data mapping for drivers, and an SOS button that instantly alerts camp security.
Slide 4: Technology & Trust
Headline: Hyper-Local, Low-Data Tech.
*USSD Integration (123#): Reaches the unbanked and offline. No smartphone or active data plan required for booking.
Optimized Architecture: App designed for intermittent connectivity. Drivers stay online and receive trips reliably on slow 2G/3G networks.
Community Validation: Hyper-local trust boosted by Boda Chairmen partnerships, providing secure, community-backed safety validation.
Slide 5: Market Opportunity
Headline: A Captive, Growing Mobility Market.
Target Users: CBO/NGO staff (needing auditable transport), local entrepreneurs (traveling to markets), and families (essential medical transit).
TAM (Total): 250,000+ residents in Kakuma, Kalobeyei, and host communities.
SAM (Serviceable): ~50,000 daily active commuters and aid workers.
SOM (Obtainable): 7,500 active users (capturing 15% of daily trips) by Year 1.
Slide 6: Competitive Advantage
Headline: The Transport Landscape: Safety vs. Affordability.
Informal Boda-Bodas: Low Safety (Unregulated) | High Affordability | Open Access.
CBO Fleets: High Safety (Regulated) | High Cost (Expensive infrastructure) | Closed Access (Staff only).
Kakumago: High Safety (Vetted, tracked) | High Affordability (Optimized digital booking) | Open Access (Built for the entire community).

Headline: Sustainable Unit Economics.
70% Core Mobility: Modest 10–15% platform fee per ride. Ensures driver loyalty and sustainable retention.

Slide 9: Go-to-Market Strategy
Headline: Solving the Chicken & Egg Problem.
Supply (Drivers): Zero-commission launch phase + subsidies for early adopters. Partnerships with Boda Chairmen for instant community trust.
Demand (Riders): M-Pesa cashback referral loops. Targeted USSD flyer campaigns at CBO gates and local markets.
Cohesive Growth: Scanning QR codes at markets and CBO gates to bridge offline-to-online user acquisition seamlessly.
